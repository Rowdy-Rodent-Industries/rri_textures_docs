# Texture Rules


> [!IMPORTANT]
> These are key rules to keep in mind, not the complete ruleset. Even approved submissions may contain issues involving color or copyrighted imagery. Flag concerns to Texture Lead before submission for evaluation and correction.

## Rules regarding use of other content

- No political, NSFW or Pinup materials

- No Copyrighted images

- No lore designs
## Rules regarding use of patterns/decals

- No Camo Patterns

- There is no set limit to how many decals or insignia you can have but it must look good and not over the top

- Keep any and all Decals to a minimum and as lore-friendly as possible

- Both Decals and Insignia must follow the colour rules, with no exceptions

- Decals and Insignia can be applied to any armour

- Decals are considered to be any small logo or symbol that are not found in the awards section of the ORBAT
## Rules regarding use of colors

- Highlights of a single Rank, Role, or squad colours may be used provided you have reached the requirements for those colours

- Custom colors should be a darker shade. Highlighters or very bright colors will not be accepted.

- NCO Grey is primarily reserved for leadership, and will have very limited quantities on nonleadership armor

- Enlisted grey may be used for nonleadership, however nonleadership will still keep the darkness of their armor within the confines of their rank. No nonleadership may go full grey

- You may use your squad color on any set of spec gear (arc,rc,jt) however you cannot use it on outside armor (P2,Pilot,Etc)

- Detachments can only use their detachment color on their detachment armor
## Rules not mentioned above.

## Faces

- For custom faces. You may choose one of the Standard Legion Clone faces, Clone Trooper A - F, or the female face provided by Texture Lead.

- Custom faces may only use 1 color.

- Face designs may only cover up to 50% of the face.

- The skin and hair color should not be altered.
## RC Armor

- Only one of the following bases may be used.

- Standard White

- Stealth

- Recpat
