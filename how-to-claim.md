> **WORKFLOW · REQUESTS**  
> How to claim approved requests, keep requesters updated, and submit for review.

## Claiming Textures

### Step 1: Verifying

1. Open the [SOB Discord — `custom-submissions`](https://discord.com/channels/898948967935639562/1250921896971735131) thread channel.
2. Find posts tagged **Needs to be done**. These submissions have been approved for creation.
3. Check that no other texture team member is tagged, and read the replies to ensure nobody has already completed the texture.
4. Once verified, add your tag to claim the submission.

### Step 2: During Creation

- A texture should normally take no more than **one week**, allowing for realistic time constraints.
- Periodically check in with the person who requested the texture and provide progress updates.
- Significant design changes or additions require approval. DM **Wolfen** for approval.

### Step 3: Submitting

1. Open the [RRI Discord — `texture-submissions`](https://discord.com/channels/1009176668998488164/1308937399195140116) channel.
2. Create a post with the requester's name and submission type.
3. Include a link to the original submission in the post body.
4. Attach the completed texture files:
   - **GIMP:** Attach the unmerged raw files.
   - **Substance Painter:** PNG exports may be acceptable, but retain the `.spp` file in case it is requested.
5. Add the **Pending Approval** tag.
6. Notify the requester that the texture has been submitted and is awaiting approval.

Once submitted, the development team takes over, assuming no further changes are needed.
